## 1. Objective and usable result

Test whether actual pinned Harness² successive refinement, `seq,k=2`, adds useful development evidence beyond a fixed planner/generator/ranker given the same starting techniques and resource allowances. Answer two narrower questions:

1. Does its delivered, natively selected portfolio outperform a fixed best-of-twelve control on these four prompts?
2. What changes between its first and second composed checkpoints, and does native selection keep the first or choose the second?

Deliver a usable native two-round route, inspectable carry-forward evidence and selection, a fixed comparison, passive Inspect records, and a completely accounted saved-data report. A negative difference, unchanged strategy or selection of the first checkpoint is a valid result. This small exposed development panel cannot establish a population effect, human preference, efficiency parity or a causal benefit of recursion alone.

**Why this phase:** the completed [Phase 3A report](PHASE3A_RP2_LIVE_REPORT.md) found fixed best-of-six ahead on all four prompts: mean utility 6.325890 versus 5.794553, difference −0.531336. All four native one-step paths completed normally; selected strategies changed on two prompts. Native inference cost $12.650235 versus fixed $2.572995. These observations support retaining fixed C-N as the descriptive seed. They do not test a later round refining an earlier harness.

| Descriptive outcomes | Native distinctness, first-answer quality, validity, failure/coverage, all inference and assessment costs, role/cache/reasoning usage and measured timing where available. |

There are **four paired prompt units**, not twelve independent observations. This chosen exposed panel does not establish statistical independence or random population sampling. No significance claim, inferential confidence interval or population winner is required from this panel. Missing targets stay missing; do not pool old attempts or manufacture a complete-case winner. Any missing required checkpoint leaves the phase's recursive-refinement analysis incomplete even if a selected-final contrast can be reported descriptively.

Retain fixed C-N as the current development seed while this phase is unrun. After a complete pilot, a positive primary mean can nominate seq2 for a separately defined confirmation; a nonpositive mean retains fixed descriptively.
