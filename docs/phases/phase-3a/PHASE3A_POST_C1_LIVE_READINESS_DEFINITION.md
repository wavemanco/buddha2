## 6. Readiness acceptance and stop/review rules

Pass only when **all** conditions hold:

1. The four fresh native C-N rollouts are valid/complete; the actual INV/DOM/probe/AGG/final path, task, source/profile/runtime and request identities are demonstrated from saved evidence.
2. All three logical editor stages produce a valid, evidence-grounded decision and complete **normally before the terminal instruction**. Either a valid edit or a genuine unchanged `KEEP_BASE` qualifies. Verify the actual consumed strings/harvest and required evidence routing; a prose keyword alone is not proof. Inspect whether the notes accurately cite available evidence and distinguish editor work from solver delivery. This is a bounded contract/completion audit, not a creative-quality judgment or a requirement for improvement; no human readers are mandatory.
3. Retain native flags, effective change, model backing and authenticity classifications separately. A valid changed harvest at the terminal boundary retains its existing native eligibility classification. **It nevertheless fails this prospective normal-workflow-readiness condition.** No R2 outcome or old authenticity rule is rewritten by this stricter readiness screen.
4. All newly admitted requests have unique identities, exact allowed models/profiles, accounted usage and known results; all failures, native shape attempts and dependent noncalls are classified. There is no new unknown/pending liability or integrity hold. Preserve historical uncertainty separately.
5. Source/context/permission/blindness controls hold. Saved inspect/report replay makes zero provider/SDK/socket calls and preserves all inputs. Publish source/authority/claim bindings, model/settings, ordinary rounds/tool actions/HTTP counts, per-role usage/cost, normal/terminal outcomes and limitations in a verified local archive.

**Early engineering stop:** on the first observed forced terminal/unfinished stage, unresolved delivery/usage, wrong source/model/profile, invalid authority, boundary failure, unpriced billing category or breached admission limit, stop all subsequent outbound admissions and preserve the failure. Native parallel workers may already have settled or admitted work; settle and classify any already admitted request without sending replacements. Do not promise that no other native branch has started. A valid native shape repair may occur only under the predeclared stock two-attempt contract; it is not a fresh readiness repeat. A normally completed, evidence-grounded unchanged decision does not trigger this stop.

After completion or any stop, perform the **mandatory first-check review and close**. Freeze the observed record before considering a method/budget change. Do not reopen or rerun the task, change prompts, require an edit, substitute a model, tune from a benchmark score, or release unknown liabilities to obtain a passing result.

## 7. Exit and exclusions

- **Ready for a separately defined comparison:** this one path passes the readiness conditions. Deliver the reusable launch/report route and exact frozen method; do not start bulk generation, judging or a fixed arm.
- **Readiness not established:** the model completes an unfinished/capped path, a required decision/harvest/evidence condition fails, or a known resource limit stops execution. Report the exact failed condition and useful trace without claiming adaptation is ineffective or impossible.
- **Held:** unknown liability, source/authority/isolation failure or another integrity stop. Preserve its full liability and missingness; no automatic recovery or fresh repetition.
- **Change-required before live work:** software/control/pricing/access feasibility cannot meet this definition. Deliver the concrete counterexample and one explicit amendment; no provider calls or hidden prerequisite chain.

No fixed/ordinary-base comparison arm, benchmark judge, new task/model/effort/strategy family, full component-authoring surface, cross-task memory, successive recursion, held-out data, human collection, CrPO/MuCE or Bayesian-surprise work is included. No external publication or provider-support message is sent. No automatic R3, second readiness task, repeat-until-success or Phase 3B is authorized.
