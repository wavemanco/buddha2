## Bounded repair policy and repetition counters

Within approved OR1 scope, these repairs may proceed without another user permission request: fixture compatibility/rejection expectations, operator/path/encoding errors, loss of structured diagnostics, manifest/binding/packaging defects, and deadline/cleanup implementation bugs **when the fix preserves the existing guards, resources and clocks**. Record the observed failure, root-cause evidence, narrow correction, affected checks and independent review before admitting a repaired candidate. An expected negative case must be declared before execution, never relabelled after an unexpected failure.

| Limit | Exact counting rule |
| --- | --- |
| At most **three frozen release candidates** | Consume a candidate at the first release-integration entry point after independent prospective review. Changed effective source, runtime, policy, execution launcher or source-bound acceptance commands require the next candidate and fresh root. A failure before main/S13 still consumes that candidate. |
| At most **two main-matrix starts** | Count at the recorded main-matrix entry/admission, before its first mock generation; a failed or partial matrix counts. Record the entry before work and independently verify it. A candidate gets at most one. |
| At most **two fresh positive S13 starts** | Count at the first invocation of its actual supervised launcher, before any model work; startup failure counts. A candidate gets at most one. |
| Saved resume and extracted cold replay | At most one of each per candidate reaching that step, and at most two of each across OR1. Neither may generate new model calls. |

The main matrix and S13 are separate four-prompt mock executions: this policy permits up to four such complete-worklist starts, not merely two. Ordinary predeclared component/capacity/negative tests are separate and still time-bounded. They cannot conceal another positive full-worklist execution. Pre-freeze unit edits/checks may iterate within the engineering cap; they do not count as a release campaign, supply an accepted group or enlarge these counters.

After failure, stop that candidate's admissions, settle all exact owners and preserve the failed result before repair. Do not resume its journal as a corrected source. Continue only if the failure is repair-eligible, the next required admissions fit the counters and time, a new current-source full acceptance is feasible, and an independent review confirms the next bounded slice fits. Availability of a counter alone is not permission to start an infeasible attempt. Reaching a cap on an already admitted final attempt does not cancel it: that attempt and its permitted downstream checks/closure may finish within the time and other limits.

**Narrow observer-only exception:** a separately versioned correction to an external review tool may repeat only its affected check, within the existing replay/cold/time caps, without regenerating an identical execution. Independent prospective review must prove that the execution SourceMap/runtime/operator/source-bound commands, all twelve group records, saved outputs, archive/manifest bytes and acceptance criteria remain unchanged. Preserve the failed observer and record the new tool hash, command and actual result. This reuses the same immutable evidence; it never rebinds results to a new source or supplies a missing pass by assertion. A source-bound packager change, an altered archived cold worker/wrapper, packet modification or weaker criterion cannot silently use this exception. Those changes still require the applicable new candidate/packet and full source rules; extracted-only execution and code-origin/guard checks remain mandatory.
